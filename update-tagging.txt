Download new id tagging scheme release from https://github.com/openstreetmap/id-tagging-schema/releases
& unzip.

Build the tag matcher:

	jq <id-tagging-schema-6.16.0/dist/presets.json  'map_values({"tags": .tags , "matchScore": (.matchScore // 1.0) })|with_entries(select(.key != "point" and .key != "line" and .key != "relation"))' > presets.json

	jq <id-tagging-schema-6.16.0/dist/translations/en.json ".en.presets.presets|map_values(.name)" > en_presets.json


	jq <id-tagging-schema-6.16.0/dist/translations/en.json ".en.presets.fields|map_values(.label)|with_entries(select(.value != \"Type\"))" > en_field_names.json
