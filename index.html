Edit Smelter

What did you edit in OSM yesterday? Last week? This OSM Edit Smelter will read all your OSM summarize your OpenStreetMap contributions in a time period, and tell you how much, and what you edited

Source code is on Github: amandasaurus/osm-edit-smelter. Made by Amanda

First, you must log in via OpenStreetMap, and all us to read your “User Preferences”. This allows us to see who you are, and prevents this site showing other people your OSM edits. This website will not be all
